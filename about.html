About the company

Who we are

FLAGTECH Group began its humble voyage in 2022 with a digital B2B repairing & manufacturing network concept — growing into a market-first, innovative presence in steel and allied metals, under the aegis of Mr. JM.

Company overview

Efficiency, quality and value in equipment manufacturing

FLAGTECH Group's network maximises efficiency, quality and value in equipment manufacturing, fabrication, machining, CNC machining, die casting, foundry steel casting, heat treatment and gear-cutting technologies.

With continuous technological advancement, strategic acquisitions and planned forays into new markets, the group has made inroads into architectural and industrial sectors. Offices in the UAE & India position it to serve customers across the Middle East, Africa, Southeast Asia, North America and CIS countries — from material supplies and project management of supplies to manufacturing and services for architectural and industrial products in foundry, steel and allied metals.

Quality & safety

Quality Policy & HSE Policy

Quality Policy

FLAGTECH believes in achieving and maintaining quality standards for all its facilities and manufactured products — focused on customer satisfaction and compliance with customer requirements, strict adherence to recognised procedures, continual facility/skill upgrades, and continual improvement with high priority on operational safety.

Certifications & compliance

HSE Policy

FLAGTECH believes all accidents are preventable: safe methods and procedures, safe working conditions with safe tools and protective equipment, protection of employees and subcontractors, and practical in-house and on-site activity for maximum safety, health and environmental protection.
