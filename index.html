Who we are

Manufacturing network for steel & allied metals

Mersha Engineering Services FZE LLC began its voyage in 2022 with a digital B2B repairing & manufacturing network concept that maximises efficiency, quality and value across equipment manufacturing, fabrication, machining, CNC machining, die casting, foundry steel casting, heat treatment and gear-cutting technologies.

With offices in the UAE & India, the group serves an expanding customer base across the Middle East, Africa, Southeast Asia, North America and CIS countries — from material supplies and project management of supplies to manufacturing and services for architectural and industrial products in foundry, steel and allied metals.
